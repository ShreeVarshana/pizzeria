A little about us

Our story

We believe in good. We launched the Fresh Pan Pizza Best Excuse Awards on our Facebook fan page. Fans were given situations where they had to come up with witty and fun excuses. The person with the best excuse won the Best Excuse Badge and Pizzeria vouchers. Their enthusiastic response proved that Pizzeria's Fresh Pan Pizza is the tastiest pan pizza ever.

Ever since we launched the Tastiest Pan Pizza, people have not been able to resist the softest, cheesiest, crunchiest, butteriest Fresh Pan Pizza. They have been leaving the stage in the middle of a performance and even finding excuses to be disqualified in a football match.

Ingredients

We're ruthless about goodness. We have no qualms about tearing up a day-old lettuce leaf (straight from the farm), or steaming a baby carrot. Cut. Cut. Chop. Chop. Steam. Stir. Stir. While they're still young and fresh — that's our motto. It makes it whole a better place.

Our Chefs

They make sauces sing and salads dance. They create magic with skill, knowledge, passion, and stirring spoons (among other things). They make goodness so good, it doesn't know what to do with itself. We do though. We send it to you.
